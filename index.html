Hawaii Travel Guide

Oahu Hawaii

#1 Tickets!

The first step is to buy your ticket! Look out for tickets all year, and plan early, or else the tickets become way too expensive.

#2 Beaches

When you are in Hawaii, you MUST go to Waimea. Waimea has beutiful clear water, and many different types of fish and sea life. (Make sure to go early in the morning, or else all the parking spots will he taken!)

#3 Food!

I would recommend getting shave ice! Its more like dessert, but you must try it! If you want something salty, an easy place to get food is 7/11, the quality is so good! Get a Musubi, or Spicy Ahi Roll!
